# EmptyState

What a list shows when it has nothing in it: an icon, a short title, one sentence, and at most one button.

- `empty` centres its content inside a panel or column. `empty--inline` is the one-line form for an empty day in a list.
- **You provide:** a title that states the situation (“All clear for today”), a sentence that says what to do next, and a button only when there is a single obvious action.
- Keep it friendly and short. No illustration, no exclamation mark.
