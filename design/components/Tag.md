# Tag

A small label that says what something is: its subject, its time state, its status, its type or its priority.

- **Subject:** `tag tag--plain` with a `.dot dot--cs|physics|maths|cyber|other` and the subject name. No fill.
- **Time:** `tag--overdue`, `tag--today`, `tag--done`. Always with the icon and the word.
- **Status:** `tag--todo` (outline), `tag--progress` (outline on a neutral fill), `tag--done`.
- **Type:** `tag--hw`, `tag--assign`, `tag--project`, each with its icon, lightest to strongest tone.
- **Priority:** `.prio` with the signal icon; add `prio--high` for High. Never coloured.
- **Counts and keys:** `.badge` for counts (`badge--overdue`, `badge--today`), `.kbd` for a key.
- One tag of each family per item at most. Add `.num` when the tag holds a number.
