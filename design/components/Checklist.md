# Checklist

Steps inside an item: an assignment's parts or a project's milestones.

- `checklist__head` shows the label, “4 of 6” and a `progress` bar filled in the item's subject colour.
- Each `checklist__item` is a square `check` and its text. Ticking strikes the text through in `ink-faint`; the tick box turns `done`.
- `checklist__add` is the last line: type a step and press Enter.
- **You provide:** the steps in order and which are done. Progress everywhere else (cards, rows) is counted from this list.
- The round checkbox (`check--round`) is different: it marks the whole item done.
